# 🩺 ClinicFlow

### Every appointment, perfectly in flow.

ClinicFlow is a web application that helps a clinic run its day-to-day activity from one simple place: patients, doctors, schedules, appointments, and medical notes.

No more phone tag, paper agendas, or double bookings. Patients book in a few clicks, doctors stay in control of their time, and the clinic team always sees the full picture.

---

## ✨ Why ClinicFlow?

| For patients | For doctors | For the clinic |
|---|---|---|
| Book an appointment anytime, without calling | A clear agenda and full control of availability | One place to manage people, schedules, and appointments |
| See your upcoming visits and your history | Focus on care, not on administration | Fewer conflicts, fewer no-shows, less paperwork |

---

## 👤 For Patients

**Create your account in seconds.** Sign up with your name, email, and phone number, then log in securely.

**Book an appointment in four simple steps**
1. Choose a specialty (cardiology, dermatology, pediatrics...)
2. Choose your doctor
3. Pick a date
4. Pick a time slot that suits you

Select the type of visit (regular consultation, follow-up, or urgent) and tell the doctor why you are coming.

**Stay in control of your visits**
- See all your upcoming appointments in one list
- Change or reschedule an appointment when your plans change
- Cancel up to 12 hours before the visit
- Keep a full history, including cancelled visits

**Your health record, always within reach**
- Keep your personal and medical details up to date
- Review your medical history after each consultation

---

## 🩺 For Doctors

**A personal agenda.** See the day, the week, and every upcoming patient at a glance.

**Your schedule, your rules.**
- Set your working days and hours
- Define the periods during which your schedule applies
- Declare your absences and leave, and the system immediately stops offering those times to patients

**Smart time slots.** ClinicFlow builds the available slots for you automatically. It takes care of:
- The length of each consultation
- A short break between patients
- Your lunch break
- Days when the clinic is closed
- Slots that are too close to the current time

**Medical notes made simple.** After a consultation, write your diagnosis and notes. Once validated, a note is locked, so the medical record stays reliable and trustworthy.

---

## 🏥 For the Clinic Team

**Administrators** manage the whole clinic:
- Patients and doctors
- Departments (Medicine, Surgery, Pediatrics, Emergency...)
- Specialties (Cardiology, Neurology, Dermatology...)
- User accounts, including the ability to deactivate an account

**Staff members** keep the clinic running:
- View the planning of the clinic
- Follow and manage appointments
- Reschedule visits when needed

---

## 🛡️ Built to Be Reliable

- **No double bookings.** Two patients can never get the same slot with the same doctor.
- **Only real availability.** Appointments can only be booked when the doctor is actually available.
- **Fair notice.** Appointments must be booked at least 2 hours ahead, and cancelled at least 12 hours ahead.
- **Private by design.** Each person only sees what their role allows: patients, doctors, staff, and administrators each have their own space.
- **Protected medical records.** Validated notes cannot be modified afterwards.
- **Secure access.** Disabled accounts cannot log in, and every email address is unique.

## 🗺️ A Typical Day with ClinicFlow

> **Sarah** has a skin problem. She logs in, picks *Dermatology*, chooses Dr. Amrani, and books Thursday at 10:30. Her appointment is confirmed instantly.
>
> **Dr. Amrani** sees the visit appear in his agenda. After the consultation, he marks it as done and writes a short note with his diagnosis.
>
> **Sarah** later finds the visit and the note in her history. If she had needed to cancel, she could have done so up to 12 hours before.
